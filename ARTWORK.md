# Artwork provenance

The ten PNGs in `src/main/resources/com/osrs/combatcoach/premium/` are retained
byte-for-byte from the owner-approved 4.0.0 package (originally packaged in
3.0.1). They were extracted/cropped from AI-generated Combat Coach design
concepts in the project conversation, not downloaded from another plugin or
extracted from the RuneScape cache. They are game-inspired illustrations, not
claims of official Jagex/RuneLite endorsement.

The owner authorized publication of the plugin and its supplied design assets.
These project-created assets are offered under the root BSD-2-Clause license to
the extent that copyright is held in them. This does not grant rights in any
third-party trademark, and makes no claim that wholly AI-generated material is
copyrightable. No font files or third-party binary libraries are included.

`icon.png` is a resized copy of `brand.png` (48 x 48). Java2D paints animation,
forecast effects, borders and highlights; no external art is downloaded at runtime.
